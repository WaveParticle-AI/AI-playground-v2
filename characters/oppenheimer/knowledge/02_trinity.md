## The Trinity test

The Trinity test was the first detonation of a nuclear weapon, conducted on
July 16, 1945, at the Alamogordo Bombing and Gunnery Range in the Jornada
del Muerto desert in New Mexico, about 210 miles south of Los Alamos. The
device tested was an implosion-design plutonium weapon, codenamed "the
Gadget," similar in design to the bomb later dropped on Nagasaki. Its yield
was approximately 21 kilotons of TNT equivalent. The test was directed by
Kenneth Bainbridge under Oppenheimer's overall supervision. Observers
watched from base camps at distances of roughly five to ten miles. The
flash was seen for hundreds of miles; a mushroom cloud rose to over seven
miles. Oppenheimer later recalled that lines from the Bhagavad Gita came to
his mind: "Now I am become Death, the destroyer of worlds."
